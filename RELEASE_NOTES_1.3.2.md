# Debra MIDI Player 1.3.2

## 🎻 FFXIV Instrument System (new!)

Your bard now handles instruments for you:

- **Auto-equip on Play** — when a song starts, Debra equips your instrument through the Hypnotoad plugin. No plugin? The picker still shows you which instrument to open in the Perform menu.
- **Instrument picker** in the FFXIV panel — "Auto" equips your default instrument (Harp out of the box, changeable in Settings), or pick any of the 28 instruments, sorted the musical way: Harp, Piano, strings, winds, brass, percussion.
- **Remembered per song** — pick an instrument once and that song will always start with it, alongside your saved track mix and octave.
- **Live switching** — change the instrument while a song plays: Debra pauses, leaves the performance, equips the new instrument and resumes right where you were.
- **Electric guitar tone changes** 🎸 — MIDI files with program changes (27–31, the same convention as other bard players) now switch guitar tones automatically mid-song: Overdriven, Clean, Muted, Power Chords, Special. Instant through Hypnotoad, or via your in-game "Tone" keybinds without it (configurable in Settings).
- **Auto-equip checkbox** — turn it off and Debra never touches your instrument.

## 🖱️ Double-Click Now Prepares

Double-clicking a song (any list — Library, Catalogue, Community, Favorites, Playlists) now **loads it ready at 0:00** instead of playing immediately: adjust the track mixer, pick your instrument, then press Play. The ▶ button on each row still plays instantly.

## 🔔 Smoother Updates

- When a new version is available, the **download window now opens right at startup** — no more missing updates.
- After the download finishes, Debra **opens the folder with the archive selected and closes itself**, so the extraction never fights a locked exe.

## 🛠️ Fixes

- **Community filters** — fixed a flicker loop where the list kept flipping between "All songs" and a genre, making songs impossible to select.
- **Localization safety net** — English texts are now embedded in the app itself, so a partial update can never show raw text keys again.
- Instrument names now match the in-game Perform menu (e.g. "Piano").

## 🌍 Languages

All 10 languages fully translated: English, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, Português, 日本語, 中文, Tiếng Việt, العربية.

💜 Thank you to everyone testing and reporting — this update is shaped by your feedback!
